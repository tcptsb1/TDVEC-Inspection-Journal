Setup, transfer and safe storage
  1. Export a full archive from the master workstation and Import & Merge it on mobile to share the same Project ID.
  2. Open this app in a full browser. A file-preview app may not execute HTML. If needed, ask IT to host this file on an approved HTTPS site.
  3. Export a selected date or range and transfer the original ZIP through an approved channel. Import & Merge on the workstation, review conflicts and export a new full archive.

Browser data is working storage only. New files, browsers or addresses may have separate storage. No automatic sync or direct folder writing. Deletions are local and do not propagate through merge. Archives contain journal.json and separate JPEGs. Keep original photos separately where required. This app uses no external libraries or upload endpoints.
